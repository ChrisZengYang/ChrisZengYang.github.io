A div element is a generic seperator of content, allowing for stylization of entire sections and classes

You can "target" a div element with the following code: document.querySelector("div"); or document.getElementById("myDivID");

A div element is very versatile and can be used for all sorts of things, including text bodys, cards, sections, and other stylizations.

A border property edits the borders of a block element, like a <div> tag. A few examples: border: 2px solid black; border-radius: 15px; border-width: 2px;
