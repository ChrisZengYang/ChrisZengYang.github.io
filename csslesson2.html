What is a div element?

A div element is a generic seperator of content, allowing for stylization of entire sections and classes

What is one way you can identify or "target" a div element?

You can "target" a div element with the following code: document.querySelector("div"); or document.getElementById("myDivID");

Why would you use a div element?

A div element is very versatile and can be used for all sorts of things, including text bodys, cards, sections, and other stylizations.

What is a border property?

A border property edits the borders of a block element, like a <div> tag.

What are some of the different border properties?

  • border: 2px solid black;
  • border-radius: 15px;
  • border-width: 2px;

What is the difference between margins and padding?

Padding is used to control the space between the content and its border, while margin is used to control spaces between elements.
